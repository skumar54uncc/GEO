# Intero Digital

Primary site: [interodigital.com](https://www.interodigital.com/). Captured 2026-10-14. Offering: full-service digital agency. GEO is a named practice, Intero GRO, inside a broader SEO, paid, content, and Amazon offer. Included because the user asked for this firm, and because the site shows how a large generalist agency productizes GEO without becoming a measurement company.

## Three-line summary

Intero sells Generative Retrieval Optimization (GRO) as the shift from ranking to being recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, and Gemini. The homepage states “400+ brands already optimized through Intero GRO,” plus two industry-style statistics that are not linked to a study in the extract. Proof points on the same page are mostly classic SEO and revenue case studies. The site is WordPress behind Cloudflare bot checks. Rank Math generates a long `llms.txt`.

## Offering

The homepage is a full-service agency: discovery, roadmap, execution across SEO, paid, content, web, and creative ([homepage](https://www.interodigital.com/)). Search and AI visibility includes SEO, GEO, local, national, technical, and ecommerce SEO. Other lines: digital PR, paid media, social ads, Amazon. They state Google Premier Partner “top 3% globally,” Microsoft Advertising Partner elite status, and a Clutch “#1 SEO and GEO Agency 2026” badge. Phone and a strategy-session form are the commercial path. JSON-LD Organization name is “Intero Digital.”

GRO, in their words, “ensures your brand is contextually understood, retrievable, and recommended” by AI systems. The diagram labels are AI, SEO, content, trust, authority, citations. A blog index on the homepage points at pieces about how AI engines evaluate B2B manufacturers, topical authority, and “the new AI search measurement stack” (3 September 2026). Those posts were not fetched, so their frameworks are not summarized. The FAQ schema defines GEO as optimizing for visibility in answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews.

## SEO overlap

GEO is a bullet under search, next to local and technical SEO. The agency’s reported proof is still revenue, traffic, rankings, and ROAS. That is the honest current state of a generalist: the new offer is GRO, the case-study archive is SEO and paid. A platform founder should expect this buyer to ask for one dashboard that does not make the SEO team look obsolete.

## Pipeline and architecture

**Facts.** HAR: 93 requests to `www.interodigital.com`, 51 to `challenges.cloudflare.com`, plus Rank Math-style markup. JSON-LD types included Organization, WebSite, and FAQPage (REA evidence `ev_8d9759a15871134752d0c5ce12a8c61c46642f39e026a270b55535e1fa1a841e`). `llms.txt` returned 200 and the body says “Generated by Rank Math SEO.” `robots.txt` disallows `/wp-admin/` except `admin-ajax.php`, sets `Crawl-delay: 10`, and points at a sitemap index. Many third-party tags did not get a clean run because Cloudflare challenged the browser.

**Inferences.** WordPress and Rank Math and Cloudflare are observed. Crawl-delay is aimed at polite bots and is ignored by several large crawlers; it is not an AI policy. The GRO delivery system is an account team. No SaaS API was observed.

## Strengths

GRO is named and defined in schema, so a model can quote it. The engine list in the FAQ is explicit. They did not pretend the Nomadix and Blackbaud stories are ChatGPT stories. Rank Math `llms.txt` is large and points at the sitemap. Crawl-delay and a disallow for wp-admin are ordinary and fine.

## Weaknesses

The two AI Overview percentages are unsourced on the page, and unsourced industry stats are how this category gets sloppy. GRO has no public method comparable to Optimist’s fan-out study or Chilli Fruit’s Chatbeat writeup. Case-study client binding in the HTML is easy to misread. Cloudflare challenges make the site hard for agents, which fights the `llms.txt` they publish. Crawl-delay is not a strategy.

## What to copy or beat

Copy a one-sentence GEO definition in FAQ schema. Beat the unsourced 60% and 35% lines by refusing to export a statistic that has no study URL. Give generalist agencies a snapshot product that is a real sample (engines, n, locale) rather than a form. Do not make them abandon the SEO case studies; join them.

## Inferences, separated

WordPress, Rank Math, and Cloudflare are observed. Industry percentages are quotations of the homepage, not findings of this project. Client-to-metric binding is flagged where the extract was ambiguous.
